NorthStar Bank(fictional) is a mid-to-large retails and commercial bank offering savings accounts, loans, credit cards, digital banking and payment services across multiple channels. Over the years, the bank has expanded its technology landscape by introducing several specilized systems for customer onboarding, payments, lending, CRM, risk assessment, fraud detection and regulatory reporting.

While each application addresses specific business capabilities, they have been developed and integrated independently, resulting in a fragmented integration landscape. Many systems communicate through point-to-point interfaces, making overall architecture increasingly difficult to maintain, scale and govern.

as the bank accelerates its digital transformation initiatives, there is a growing need for a standardized integration platform that enables secure and scalable communication across across business application while supporting both synchronous and asynchronous interactions. 

---
### Current Challenges

The existing integration landscape presents several technical and operation challenges:
#### Point-to-point Integration Sprawl
  Each new system requires additional custom integrations, increasing development and maintenance effort.

#### Tight Coupling
Changes in one application often impact multiple connected systems, creating issues while deployment

#### Limited Process Visibility
Business process spanning multiple systems make  end-to-end orchestration difficult by reducing visibility into transaction status
#### Inconsitent Security Controls
Authentication, authorization and auditing are implemented differently across services
#### Difficult to scale
Curent architecture struggles to accommodate increasing transaction volumens driven by new business requirements

#### High Operation Load
Lack of centralized monitoring and error handling makes it chanllenging to address errors and also increase burden on support teams to troubleshoot across different stacks and integration mechanisms.

### Objectives

Develop an **Enterprise Integration Hub** to modernize its integration landscape and replace frageented point-to-point communication with a standardized integration architecture. 

The platform should provide 
  - API Management
  - Workflow Orchestration
  - Event-driven Messaging
  - Centralized Monitoring 

to improve interoperability between enterprise applications. 

This unified integration approach aims to reduce complexity, improve operation resilience, accelerate onboarding of new systems and support future digital transformation initiatives. 

---
### Scope
- Define a centralized integration architecture that standarizes communication across systems

### Assumptions
- Core Banking systems remain unchanged
- Existing business capabilities continue to operate
- Integration Hub acts as an integration and orchestration layer rather than replacing business applications.
