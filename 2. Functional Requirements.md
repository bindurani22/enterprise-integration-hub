### Functional Requirements

#### API Management
The platform shall expose banking capabilities through standardized APIs.
Requirements:
- Publish APIs for customer, account, payment, loan and other microservices
- Route requests to appropriate backend systems
- Support API versioning
- Enfore rate limiting and throttling policies

#### Workflow Orchestration
The platform shall orchestrate multi-step banking processes.
Requirements:
- Execute long-running business workflows
- Track workflow transition state and progress
- Invoke multiple downstream systems
- Maintain business rules
- Support manual approval tasks, routing logic and SLAs
- Handle compensation actions 

#### Event Management
The platform shall publish and consume events generated by banking systems and internal and external services.
Requirements:
- Publish and consume real-time events to streaming platforms
- Support asynchronous communication
- Persit event data for tracebility
- Support multiple event consumers

#### Service Integration 
The platform shall integrate with systems in the existing landscape.
Requirements:
- Connect to internal and external systems
- Support synchronous, asynchronous types of communication
- Handle different protocols - HTTP, FTP
- Provide reusable integration capabilities

#### Service Discovery
The platform shall dynamically route requests to backend services.
Requirements:
- Register available services
- Supports service discovery 
- Route traffic based on rules and policies
- Support green-blue and canary deployments

#### Authentication
The platform shall authenticate and authorize all requests before processing.
Requirements:
- Integrate with banks security landscape - IdPs
- Autheticate and authorize access to APIs and services
- Support customer, employee and partner access model
- Implement authentication failure retry limit and backoff strategy

#### Notification
The platform shall facilitate customer notifications.
Requirements:
- Trigger notifications from workflow and events
- Support email, SMS and push channels
- Track notification delivery status
- Support configurable notification templates

#### Analytics
The platform shall provide integration and business insights.
Requirements:
- Integrate metric with existing analytics platform
- Stream business events to analytics platforms
- Support KPI dashboards
- Generate audit and compliance reports
